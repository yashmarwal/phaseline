Phase AI may only state what is written in this file. Anything not here is unknown, and the answer is to point to the team.

## Who we are
- Phaseline builds custom websites, private business systems and custom software (including AI) for Indian B2B businesses: manufacturers, exporters, traders and service businesses.
- Our team is based in Delhi, India. We work with businesses across India.
- This website is a marketing site, not a shop.
- You talk directly to the people building your project. No junior handlers, no outsourcing.

## Services
- **Web Presence** (the website service): a custom website that presents your business with the credibility serious buyers expect. Bespoke design built around your brand, clear presentation of your products, capabilities and work, enquiries delivered straight to your WhatsApp and email, and a search-ready structure so customers can find you on Google. We do not promise rankings. Typical timeline: 3 to 4 weeks.
- **Business Systems** (the operations service): a private operations platform that replaces registers, notebooks and scattered chats. An admin panel built around how your team works, tracking of the records your business runs on with status, notes and reminders, workflows designed around how you operate, and enquiries delivered to WhatsApp and email. Typical timeline: 5 to 8 weeks, depending on scope.
- **Custom Software & AI** (the tailored software service): purpose-built software for one specific problem that no off-the-shelf tool solves. AI is built in where it adds real value, including assistants trained on your own information. Scoped and quoted after an initial consultation. Timeline is set after the consultation.
- Services can be combined as a business grows.
- **Care** (optional monthly plan): ongoing content updates, security updates and search-visibility monitoring. We check that the site is up every 5 minutes and get an alert straight away if it is not. It can be cancelled with 30 days' notice.
- An AI assistant for a client's own business is available as an add-on. Online payments are quoted separately if required.

## How a project runs
- Five steps: Discovery, Design, Build, Review, Launch.
- Direct access to the team throughout. No indefinite design loops.

## Price and payment
- We do not publish prices. Every project is quoted after a short consultation, in a clear proposal.
- Unless the proposal says something different, payment is in three parts: 30% as an advance before work starts, 40% when half of the work is done, and the remaining 30% before launch.

## Support, ownership and cancellation
- Every project includes 6 months of support. Support covers fixing faults in what we delivered. New features and changes are separate work.
- The client owns the custom code and data we build. Once the project has launched and all payments due have been made, it is theirs, unless the proposal says something different. We share the code files when the client asks for them.
- If a client cancels before launch, the unfinished work is not handed over and stays with Phaseline. The client pays for the work completed up to that day. If work has not started, we refund the advance. Once work has started, advance payments and payments already made are not refunded.
- Full details are on the Terms & Conditions and Cancellation & Refunds pages.
