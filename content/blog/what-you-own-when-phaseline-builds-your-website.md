Ownership is the part of a website project that owners most often forget to ask about, and regret later. If you do not own your website, you cannot move it, change developers or even always update it. This page explains plainly what you own when Phaseline builds your website or business system, and the terms behind that. The binding terms are in your proposal and on our Terms and Conditions and Cancellation and Refunds pages. This page is a guide, not a contract.

## The short version

The website files, design files and account logins belong to your business from launch day. The custom code and data we build for you are yours once the project has launched and all payments due have been made, unless the proposal says something different. We share the code files when you ask for them. There is no vendor lock-in.

## What is included

**Website files.** The files that make up your site.

**Design files.** The designs created for your project.

**Account logins.** Access to the accounts connected to your site, so you are never locked out.

**Your domain and security certificate.** Handed over at launch, along with visitor tracking, so your site and its address are under your control. See [domain and hosting basics](/blog/domain-and-hosting-basics).

**Custom code and data.** The code we write for you and the data your system holds are yours.

## Why we do it this way

Many Indian businesses end up choosing between a template site with borrowed tools that do not fit, or an agency that charges a retainer for changes that should take an hour. We think a business should be able to leave. If you can take your website and move it, we have to earn your continued business by being useful, not by holding your files.

## Built for independence

Where your project has an admin panel, it is built so your team can update content and records without calling us for every change. Ownership is only useful if you can actually run the thing. Read about [admin panels](/blog/admin-panel-for-your-business-website).

## The conditions

To be clear and fair:

- **Payment.** Ownership of the custom code and data passes once the project has launched and all payments due have been made.
- **Your proposal.** If your proposal says something different, the proposal applies.
- **If a project is cancelled before launch.** The unfinished work is not handed over and stays with Phaseline. You pay for the work completed up to that day. If work has not started, we refund the advance. Once work has started, advance payments and payments already made are not refunded. The full details are on our cancellation and refunds page.
- **Third-party services.** Some tools you use, such as a payment gateway or a hosting provider, have their own terms. Ownership of those is between you and the provider.

## Support is separate from ownership

Every project includes 6 months of support. Support covers fixing faults in what we delivered. New features and changes are separate work. Owning your site means you can also choose who supports it afterwards. If you want ongoing help from us, see [Phaseline Care explained](/blog/phaseline-care-explained).

## Questions to ask any developer

Whoever you hire, ask these and get the answers in writing:

1. Who owns the code and the design files?
2. In whose name are the domain and hosting?
3. Will I receive all logins at launch?
4. Can I take the site to another developer?
5. What happens to my data if I leave?
6. What happens if the project is cancelled?

Our guide to [choosing a web developer](/blog/choosing-a-web-developer-in-delhi) has more.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I get the source code?

Yes. We share the code files when you ask for them, once ownership has passed under the terms above.

### Do I own the design?

The design files for your project belong to your business from launch day.

### What if I stop working with Phaseline?

You keep what you own. You can move your site and engage another developer.
