Fitness Future Gym is a unisex gym in Nangloi, Delhi, open for 8 years. This case study is about a business system rather than a brochure website: how we replaced paper with one system that handles memberships, fees and attendance. We describe the problem, what we built and why, and we do not quote revenue or retention figures, because we have not published any for this client.

## The business

A gym lives on repeat visits and monthly payments. The owner needs to know three things at any moment: who is a member, who has paid, and who has stopped coming. When those answers live in notebooks, they are slow to find and easy to miss.

## The problem

Attendance and fees were tracked on paper. As a result:

- **Overdue fees went unnoticed.** On paper, nobody sees a missed due date until someone checks the right page.
- **Trial visitors were never followed up.** A person who tried the gym and did not join simply disappeared. There was no list and no reminder.
- **There was no way to reach members between visits.** Offers and announcements depended on the member walking in.

## What we built

- **Member self sign-up and QR check-in.** Members register themselves and check in by QR code, so attendance is recorded without a register.
- **Fee tracking with automatic reminders.** Due dates are tracked per member, and reminders go out by WhatsApp and email.
- **An admin panel that flags what needs attention.** It shows overdue fees, inactive members, and trial visitors who never joined.
- **Offers sent to everyone at once.** The team can reach all members with a single message.
- **A member dashboard.** Each member gets their own dashboard with a workout planner and a food log.

## Why we made these choices

**Check-in is the source of truth.** If attendance is recorded the moment a member arrives, the data about who is active and who has lapsed builds itself. Nobody has to remember to update a register.

**Reminders belong to the system.** Chasing fees is an awkward job when it falls to a person. When reminders go out automatically on WhatsApp and email, the conversation about money starts earlier and more politely, and the owner is not the one doing it.

**Flags beat reports.** An owner does not want to read a table to find problems. The admin panel surfaces overdue fees, inactive members and unconverted trials directly, so the next action is obvious.

**Give members something back.** A workout planner and food log give members a reason to open the system themselves, which makes it more than a back-office tool.

## What other businesses can take from this

Any business with recurring customers, such as a gym, a coaching class or a club, can benefit from the same pattern: record the visit, track the due date, flag the exceptions and remind automatically. The tools differ, but the idea is the same.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a small gym use a custom system instead of off-the-shelf software?

Yes. The question is whether off-the-shelf tools fit how you work. A custom system is designed around your process and you own it, which our [custom versus off-the-shelf guide](/blog/custom-software-vs-off-the-shelf) explains further.

### How do QR check-ins work?

A member scans a QR code on arrival, and the system records the visit against their profile automatically.

### Can reminders go out on WhatsApp?

Yes. In this project, fee reminders go out by WhatsApp and email based on each member's due date.
