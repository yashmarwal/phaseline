A website is a living thing. After launch it needs checking, updating and caring for. Phaseline Care is our optional monthly add-on for that. This page explains exactly what it includes, how it differs from the support that comes with every project and who it suits. For the general principles, see our guide to [website maintenance](/blog/website-maintenance-and-care).

## What it is

Phaseline Care is monthly care for your website or system. It is an add-on after launch. It can be added to any of our services, and you can cancel with 30 days' notice.

## What is included

**Uptime checks every 5 minutes.** We check that your site is up every 5 minutes, day and night. If it goes down, we are alerted and respond during working hours. We say "respond during working hours" deliberately: we do not claim round-the-clock repairs, but we do not leave you finding out from a customer.

**Monthly content and security updates.** Each month we make content updates and apply security updates.

**A check on how you appear on Google.** A regular look at how your site appears in search. This is a check, not a promise of rankings.

**A quarterly review.** Every quarter, a review of how your site looks and loads.

## How it differs from the included support

Every Phaseline project includes 6 months of support. Support covers fixing faults in what we delivered. New features and changes are separate work.

Care is different: it is the ongoing monthly routine of watching, updating and reviewing, rather than fixing faults in the original build. Many clients use the included support first and add Care when it is about to end, or from the start. It is your choice.

## Who it suits

- **Businesses without anyone technical** who still want the site watched.
- **Sites that change often,** where monthly content updates save time.
- **Businesses for whom downtime costs enquiries,** where knowing quickly matters.
- **Owners who want a regular outside look** at speed and appearance.

## Who might not need it

If your team updates the site itself through an admin panel, handles hosting and security, and checks uptime with its own tools, you may not need Care. That is fine. Ownership means you decide. See [what you own](/blog/what-you-own-when-phaseline-builds-your-website).

## Why it matters

Most website failures are quiet: a form that stops delivering, a certificate that expires, an update that was never applied. The point of care is to catch them early. See [website maintenance: what it is and what to look for](/blog/website-maintenance-and-care) for the wider picture.

## What it does not do

To be clear:

- It is not a guarantee of rankings or traffic.
- It does not include new features or large changes. Those are separate work.
- It is not a promise of immediate fixes at any hour. Responses happen during working hours.

## How to add it

Mention it when you discuss your project, or ask for it after launch through our [contact page](/contact). We will confirm what applies to your site and quote it. We do not publish prices.

## How we think about it

We believe a business should be able to rely on its website without thinking about it. Care is how we offer that for clients who want it, without locking anyone in: it is a monthly add-on you can cancel.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I add Care to a website someone else built?

Ask us. The answer depends on how the site is built and hosted.

### What happens if my site goes down?

We are alerted by the 5-minute checks and respond during working hours.

### Do I have to take Care?

No. It is optional, and can be added to any service after launch.
