A website that attracts visitors but fails to turn them into enquiries is a brochure nobody reads. The last step, the moment a buyer decides to get in touch, is where many websites lose people. In India, that step increasingly happens on WhatsApp. This guide covers how to design enquiry forms and WhatsApp buttons that buyers use and that reach you reliably.

## Why WhatsApp matters

Most Indian business conversations already happen on WhatsApp. Buyers are comfortable with it, they have it open and a message there feels lower-stakes than a phone call or a formal form. For a trader or manufacturer, getting enquiries where the team already works means no new habit to learn and faster replies. For several of our clients, enquiries from the website land straight on WhatsApp.

## Principles for a good WhatsApp button

**Make it visible without being annoying.** A button that follows the visitor on mobile is useful. One that covers content or pops up repeatedly is not.

**Prefill the message.** WhatsApp lets you start a conversation with a message already written, such as "Hi, I'd like a quote for [product]". A prefilled message tells you what the buyer wants and saves them typing. On a product page, include the product name.

**Put the right number behind it.** Use the number your team actually monitors, and check it regularly. A button that points to an abandoned number loses every enquiry.

**Say what will happen.** A short label such as "Request a quote on WhatsApp" is clearer than an icon alone.

**Work on desktop too.** On computers, the button should open WhatsApp Web or prompt the app, so desktop visitors are not stranded.

## Principles for a good enquiry form

A form is still valuable. Some buyers want a written record, some work on corporate devices, and forms capture structured details.

**Ask for the minimum.** Name, a way to contact them and what they need. Every extra field reduces completion. You can ask more once the conversation starts.

**Match the fields to your trade.** For a bulk order, a design, quantity and colour are worth asking. For a service, a short description of the need.

**Explain what happens next.** "We reply within one working day" is reassuring, but only if you keep to it.

**Confirm the submission.** Show a clear thank-you message so the buyer knows it worked.

**Make it easy on a phone.** Large tap targets, the right keyboard for each field and no tiny text.

## Put contact paths where decisions happen

Do not hide them on a Contact page alone. Place them:

- **On every product,** so a buyer can ask about exactly what they are looking at. See how this works in the [Mapps Creation case study](/blog/mapps-creation-fabric-catalogue-website).
- **In the header or footer,** so they are always reachable.
- **After key information,** such as pricing notes or specifications.
- **On the Contact page,** with every option together: WhatsApp, phone, email, address and form.

## Make sure every enquiry reaches you

The most expensive failure is a form that looks fine but delivers nothing. Protect against it.

1. **Test end to end.** Submit the form yourself and confirm where the message lands.
2. **Send to more than one place.** For example, both WhatsApp and email.
3. **Track enquiries.** Do not leave them scattered across chats. See [enquiry and lead tracking](/blog/enquiry-and-lead-tracking-system).
4. **Check regularly,** at least monthly. Forms break when software changes.

## Respond quickly

A fast reply is part of the design. Buyers contact several suppliers at once, and the first useful answer often wins the conversation. Decide who watches the number and how quickly they reply, and keep to it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should I use a form or a WhatsApp button?

Both. WhatsApp suits quick, informal contact and a form captures structured details and a written record.

### Can WhatsApp enquiries be tracked?

Not automatically. If you want a record, use a system that captures enquiries and lets you add status and notes.

### Is it safe to show my phone number on a website?

It is common practice for businesses and expected by buyers. If you worry about unwanted messages, use a business number and consider the features WhatsApp Business offers.
