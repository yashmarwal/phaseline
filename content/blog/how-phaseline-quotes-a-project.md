If you have looked at our services page, you will have noticed there are no price tags. That is deliberate, and this page explains why and how a quote is prepared. We think you should know how pricing works before you speak to us. The binding terms are in your proposal and on our Terms and Conditions and Cancellation and Refunds pages.

## Why we do not publish prices

A price on a page is a guess about a stranger's project. A five-page website and a catalogue with an admin panel, customer accounts and online payments are very different amounts of work. A single published number would be too high for some, too low for others and misleading for everyone. Instead, every project is quoted after a short consultation, in a clear proposal.

For the factors that drive cost, read [what sets website cost in India](/blog/website-development-cost-india).

## How a quote is prepared

1. **A conversation.** A short consultation or feasibility audit to understand your business, your buyers and your goal. See [what to expect from a feasibility audit](/blog/feasibility-audit-what-to-expect).
2. **A scope.** What will be built, in plain language: pages, features, admin tools and anything that is not included.
3. **A timeline.** A website typically takes 3 to 4 weeks, and a business system 5 to 8 weeks, depending on scope. Custom software is timed after a consultation.
4. **A proposal.** The scope, the timeline, the price and the payment terms, in writing.

You can ask questions, adjust the scope and compare the proposal with others before deciding.

## What affects the price

- The number and complexity of pages.
- Whether there is an admin panel, and what it manages.
- Whether your business needs workflows, records or reminders.
- Whether you need online payments, which are quoted separately if required.
- Whether an AI assistant is included, as an add-on.
- How much content has to be produced.
- Any integrations or custom behaviour.

## How payment works

Unless your proposal says something different, payment is in three parts:

- **30% as an advance,** before work starts.
- **40% when half of the work is done.**
- **The remaining 30% before launch.**

The structure ties payment to progress, so you pay as the work moves forward.

## What is included

Every project includes 6 months of support, which covers fixing faults in what we delivered. New features and changes are separate work. Ownership of the website files, design files and logins passes at launch, and the custom code and data are yours once the project has launched and all payments due have been made. See [what you own](/blog/what-you-own-when-phaseline-builds-your-website). Care is an optional monthly add-on; see [Phaseline Care explained](/blog/phaseline-care-explained).

## If you cancel

Fair terms matter on both sides. In summary:

- If work has not started, we refund your advance.
- Once work has started, advance payments and payments already made are not refunded.
- If you cancel before launch, the unfinished work is not handed over and stays with Phaseline, and you pay for the work completed up to that day.

The details are on our cancellation and refunds page, and your proposal governs if it says something different.

## How to compare our quote with others

Put proposals side by side. Compare scope, timeline, ownership, support and payment terms, not only the total. A lower price with less included or with unclear ownership is not necessarily cheaper. Our guide to [choosing a web developer](/blog/choosing-a-web-developer-in-delhi) lists the questions to ask.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I get a rough price before a call?

Prices depend on scope, so we ask a few questions first. The call is complimentary.

### Is the quote fixed?

The proposal states the scope and price. Changes outside that scope are agreed and priced separately.

### Do you charge for revisions?

Revisions within the agreed design process are part of the project. Changes outside the scope are separate. Your proposal states what applies.
