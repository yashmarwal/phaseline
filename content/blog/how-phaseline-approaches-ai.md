Everyone is adding AI to everything, and a lot of it is hype. Our approach is deliberately modest: use AI where it genuinely helps, tell people plainly when they are talking to it, and never let it say more than the business can stand behind. This page explains how that works on our own site and in the assistants we build for clients.

## Phase AI on our own site

Our website has a chat assistant called Phase AI. It is how we practise what we describe to clients. A few principles shape it.

**It may only state what we have written down.** Phase AI works from a fact sheet that we maintain about our company, services and process. Anything not in it is treated as unknown, and the right answer is to point to the team.

**Answers to common questions are reviewed.** The most frequent questions, such as how a project runs or how long a website takes, are answered in wording we have checked, so what you read is consistent.

**It does not invent prices, promises or rankings.** We do not publish prices, so it does not make them up. It points you to a consultation instead.

**It is an assistant, and it is presented as one.** You should know you are chatting with software.

**It points you to the team.** It is built to move visitors towards talking to the team on WhatsApp or email.

## How chats are handled

Our privacy policy describes this in detail. In summary, messages you type into the chat, and any details you give in its short form, are stored in a private database. Phase AI chats are kept for about a week, then emailed to our team as a spreadsheet and deleted from the database. The policy also lists the service providers involved. If you have concerns, read the policy or ask us before you use the chat.

We mention this because assistants that capture visitor conversations should be transparent about it, and because it is the standard we recommend to clients. See [AI assistant accuracy and risks](/blog/ai-assistant-accuracy-and-risks).

## How we build assistants for clients

Our AI assistant integration is an add-on to our website and business system services, and part of our custom software and AI service. We build a custom assistant that answers customer questions from your own business information and qualifies leads. In practice we:

1. **Write the knowledge with you.** The facts it may state, in plain language. See [what to put in an AI assistant's knowledge](/blog/ai-assistant-knowledge-base-guide).
2. **Set its limits.** What it must not say or promise.
3. **Decide where leads go,** such as WhatsApp or email.
4. **Test it on hard questions** before launch.
5. **Hand it over,** with the knowledge and data belonging to your business.

For more on whether it fits your business, read the [AI chatbot guide](/blog/ai-chatbot-for-business-website) and the comparison of [WhatsApp bots, Meta's business AI and custom assistants](/blog/whatsapp-bot-vs-meta-business-ai-vs-custom-assistant).

## What we will not promise

- **Perfect accuracy.** No AI system can promise it. We reduce errors with careful information and limits, and we recommend regular review.
- **That AI will replace your team.** It handles first questions. People handle relationships.
- **Results.** We do not guarantee leads, sales or rankings from any AI feature.

## Where AI does not belong

AI is not the answer to every problem. A clear website, an easy way to enquire and a fast reply from a person often matter more. We would rather tell you an assistant is not worth it for your business than sell you one. That honesty is part of the approach.

## Other uses of AI

AI can help behind the scenes too, for example in drafting, summarising or organising information. Whatever is published under a business's name should be checked by a person who stands behind it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Phase AI a human?

No. It is an AI assistant. A person is available through WhatsApp and email.

### Does Phase AI know everything about Phaseline?

It only knows what is in our fact sheet. For anything else, it points you to the team.

### Can you build a Phase AI for my business?

We build custom assistants trained on your own business information, as an add-on to our services.
