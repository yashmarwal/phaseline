If you want AI to help answer customer questions, you now have several routes. You can set up a bot on WhatsApp, use the AI features Meta provides for business accounts or have a custom assistant built into your own website. Each has real strengths, and the right choice depends on where your customers talk to you and how much control you want. This guide compares them honestly. Features and availability change quickly, so check current details with each provider before you decide.

## Where do your customers talk to you?

Start here, because it decides most of the answer.

- If most conversations happen on **WhatsApp**, the assistant should be where your customers already are.
- If buyers mostly **find you through your website** and ask questions there, an assistant on the site makes sense.
- Many businesses have both, and it is reasonable to use more than one route.

## Option 1: A WhatsApp bot

A bot on the WhatsApp Business platform replies to messages automatically, from simple menus and quick replies to more advanced AI-driven answers.

**Strengths:**
- Meets customers on the channel they already use.
- Good for repeated questions, order status and quick replies.
- Familiar to Indian customers.

**Things to watch:**
- Rules and costs for business messaging are set by the platform and can change. Check the current terms.
- Setup can require approvals and a business account.
- The conversation lives in a chat, which is hard to track unless connected to a system.

## Option 2: Meta's business AI

Meta offers AI features for business accounts across its apps, designed to answer common customer questions on your behalf.

**Strengths:**
- Provided by the platform, so setup can be lighter.
- Works inside apps your customers already use.

**Things to watch:**
- You are working within Meta's product, features and rules. You have less control over behaviour and presentation.
- Availability varies by region and account type, and changes over time.
- Your information must still be accurate and complete, or its answers will be too.

## Option 3: A custom assistant on your website

A custom assistant is built by a team and trained on your own information, then placed on your site. It can be tuned to your tone, your rules and your lead-capture process.

**Strengths:**
- **Control.** You decide what it can say, what it must not say and how it hands over to a person.
- **Fit.** It can ask the questions that matter in your trade and pass details to the right place.
- **Ownership.** The knowledge and conversations are yours.
- **It lives on your site,** where buyers who do not yet know you are making decisions.

**Things to watch:**
- It costs more upfront than switching on a platform feature.
- It needs maintenance to stay current.
- It serves website visitors, so you need another route for WhatsApp.

## A quick comparison

- **Where it lives:** a WhatsApp bot lives on WhatsApp, Meta's business AI lives inside Meta's apps and a custom assistant lives on your website.
- **Control over answers:** moderate for a WhatsApp bot, limited for Meta's business AI, high for a custom assistant.
- **Setup effort:** moderate for a WhatsApp bot, lower for Meta's business AI, higher for a custom assistant.
- **Fit for unusual processes:** moderate for a WhatsApp bot, limited for Meta's business AI, best for a custom assistant.

## A practical way to choose

1. **List the ten questions customers ask most.** Can any option answer them accurately?
2. **Decide how important control is.** If wrong answers carry a high cost, favour options where you control the information.
3. **Think about leads.** Where should details go: a chat, an email or a tracker?
4. **Start small.** You can begin with one route and add another.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I use more than one?

Yes. Many businesses run a website assistant and a WhatsApp option, ideally with consistent information.

### Do these tools cost money to run?

Typically yes, in different ways: platform fees, usage charges or maintenance. Ask each provider for current pricing and terms.

### Which is best for a small manufacturer?

It depends on where your buyers contact you. If they come through your site and then move to WhatsApp, a website assistant that hands over to WhatsApp can work well.
