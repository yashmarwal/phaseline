Mapps Creation

Manufacturing & Screen Printing PROJECT // 02 · Jaipur - Rajasthan

Tanish Creation

A 65-year-old Jaipur screen printing house that now takes bulk fabric orders online.

Before

Orders and quotes started with a phone call or a visit, and buyers could not see the designs, the minimum order or how to book a factory visit beforehand.

What we built

A bulk order form where buyers pick a design, quantity and colour and send the request straight to WhatsApp, the minimum order shown on every design, a factory-visit booking form, and the full design library online.

Handmade Leather & Paper Exporter PROJECT // 04 · Sanganer - Jaipur

INS Overseas

A Jaipur handmade leather journal and paper exporter, now showing its full range to overseas wholesale buyers.

Before

Buyers abroad could not browse the range or see minimum orders without asking, and the team had no simple way to keep products and photos up to date.

What we built

A catalogue of seven product ranges with the minimum order on every card and an Enquire button, a wholesale partner page, WhatsApp and email enquiry, and a private admin where the team uploads photos and manages products, the gallery and the homepage themselves.

Fitness & Community PROJECT // 06 · Nangloi - Delhi

Fitness Future Gym

A unisex gym in Nangloi, Delhi, open for 8 years, that now runs memberships, fees and attendance from one system.

Before

Attendance and fees were tracked on paper, so overdue fees went unnoticed, trial visitors were never followed up, and there was no way to reach members between visits.

What we built

Members sign up themselves and check in by QR code. Fee due dates are tracked per member with automatic WhatsApp and email reminders. The admin panel flags overdue fees, inactive members and trial visitors who never joined, and sends offers to everyone at once. Members get their own dashboard with a workout planner and food log.
