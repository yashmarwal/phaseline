How a Phaseline project runs, from first call to launch

The five steps of a Phaseline project, discovery, design, build, review and launch, what happens in each, what we need from you and what you receive.

Buying a website or business system is easier when you know what the process will look like. This guide explains how a Phaseline project runs: the five steps, what happens in each, what we ask of you and what you receive at the end. It matches the process described on our process page, with more detail on what to expect.

The short version

Every project follows five steps: Discovery, Design, Build, Review and Launch. A website typically takes 3 to 4 weeks, and a business system 5 to 8 weeks, depending on scope. Custom software is timed after a consultation. Throughout, you speak directly to the people building the project, with no junior handlers in between, and we respond within 1 hour during working hours.

Before step one: the conversation

Most projects begin with a short conversation, either a consultation or a feasibility audit. We learn about your business, review your current site or plans and agree whether and how we can help. You then receive a proposal. We do not publish prices, because the right price depends on scope. See how we quote a project and what to expect from a feasibility audit.

Step 1: Discovery

We learn how your business sells, who decides and exactly what gets built. This is where we ask questions about your buyers, your products, your current process and what is going wrong.

What we need from you: time for the conversation, access to anyone who knows the detail, and an honest account of what frustrates you today.

What you get: an agreed scope. The milestone at this stage is a signed scope.

Step 2: Design

We prepare page sketches and full design previews for your approval. For a system, this includes how the screens and workflows will work.

What we need from you: clear feedback, ideally consolidated from one decision-maker, and your content: text, photographs and product details. Our content checklist helps you prepare.

What you get: a design you have seen and approved before anything is built. The milestone is design approval. There are no indefinite design loops.

Step 3: Build

We build every page from your approved design, including the enquiry form. For a business system, we build the admin panel and workflows.

What we need from you: timely answers to questions, and any remaining content.

What you get: a live preview you can open and click through while it is being built.

Step 4: Review

You check every page on your phone and ours. We test the enquiry form from start to finish, so that a message sent from the site reaches the right place.

What we need from you: a careful look at everything, with changes listed together.

What you get: a tested site, with issues found and fixed before launch.

Step 5: Launch

We hand over your domain, security certificate, visitor tracking and all files. The website files, design files and account logins belong to your business from launch day. See what you own.

What you get: a live site and a clean handover.

Payment along the way

Unless your proposal says something different, payment is in three parts: 30% as an advance before work starts, 40% when half of the work is done and the remaining 30% before launch.

After launch

Every project includes 6 months of support, which covers fixing faults in what we delivered. New features and changes are separate work. You can add monthly care after launch; see Phaseline Care explained.

What makes projects go smoothly

  • One decision-maker who can approve quickly.
  • Content ready early.
  • Feedback given in one consolidated message.
  • A clear scope, changed only deliberately.

Starting

If you would like to begin, you can reach us through our contact page. We reserve build slots by month, so it helps to ask early.

Frequently asked questions

Will I deal with a project manager?

You deal directly with the people building your project.

Can I see the work before it is finished?

Yes. A live preview is available during the build, and you review every page before launch.

What if I want changes after approval?

Small changes are normal. Larger changes affect scope and timeline, so we agree them before doing the work.
