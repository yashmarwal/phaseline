The Company

Software, Systems and Websites for Indian B2B Businesses

We build websites, business systems and custom software for manufacturers, exporters, traders and service businesses across India.

Our Story

Built by doing the work, not pitching it.

Phaseline began with real client work: a fabric trader in Surat, a screen printing manufacturer and a ladies' wear maker in Jaipur, a cotton apparel brand and a gym in Delhi. Each one needed something built around how it actually runs, not a template. We built for them one at a time, and Phaseline is the team that grew out of that work.

architecture Why We Exist

The Gap We Close

Many Indian B2B businesses end up choosing between two poor options: a template site and borrowed tools that don't fit how they work, or an agency that charges a retainer for changes that should take an hour.

Phaseline exists to close that gap: a custom website, business system or piece of software that fits how you work, that you own, with 6 months of support included.
