Cookies Policy

This page explains what this website stores in your browser. The short answer is very little, and none of it is used to track you.

1.​The short version

This website does not set cookies of its own. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, so there is no cookie banner to accept or reject.

2.​What is stored in your browser

The website uses your browser's session storage for two small items. They only help the menu highlight move smoothly when you go from one page to another.

  • navIdx: remembers which item in the top menu you were on.
  • tabIdx: remembers which item in the phone menu bar you were on.

Both stay in your browser tab and are deleted when you close it. They hold a position number only, not personal information, and they are never sent to us.

We do not use local storage or persistent cookies.

3.​Fonts, icons and scripts

The fonts, icons and scripts this website uses are served from our own hosting. Loading them does not send your IP address to other companies.

4.​When you send the contact form

When you send the contact form, your browser connects to our form service, which emails your message to our team. That service can see your IP address when that happens. This only happens when you send the form, not when you browse the website. It is explained in our Privacy Policy.

5.​Phase AI chat

The Phase AI chat assistant does not store anything in your browser. Your conversation lives in the page's memory and disappears when you reload or close the page. When you ask a question that needs the AI, your browser sends it to our server, which is explained in our Privacy Policy.

6.​Server logs

Like any website, our hosting provider records basic technical data when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you open. This is explained in our Privacy Policy.

7.​Links to other services

When you click a WhatsApp link, WhatsApp opens and its own policies and cookies apply. When you click an email link, your email app opens. We do not control these services.

8.​Controlling cookies in your browser

You can see, block or delete cookies and stored site data in your browser settings. Look for "Privacy" or "Site data". You can clear session storage at any time by closing the tab. Blocking storage will not stop this website from working.

9.​Changes to this policy

If we ever add analytics or other tools that use cookies, we will update this page first and ask for your consent where the law requires it. The date at the top shows when this page last changed.

10.​Contact

Questions about this policy: connect@phaseline.in.
